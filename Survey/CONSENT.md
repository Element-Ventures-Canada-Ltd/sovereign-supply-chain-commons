# Respondent consent, confidentiality and export-control text

**Approved by counsel, October 2026.** This text is shown to every respondent before Module A.

## About this survey

This survey maps the Canadian space-launch supply chain so that gaps can be found and closed. It is run twice a year by Element Ventures (Canada) Ltd. on behalf of the program. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time.

**Purpose.** To build an aggregate picture of capabilities, dependencies and gaps; to maintain a confidential organization record where you allow it; and, where you allow it, to consider your organization for sprint and red-team invitations.

**Who uses the results.** Aggregate results are published openly. Confidential records are used only by named program staff under confidentiality agreements.

**Record linkage.** With your consent (tier 2 or 3), your answers are linked to your organization's record in the program's supply-chain data layer.

## Consent tiers

1. **Aggregate only.** Your answers are used only in statistics that cannot identify you.
2. **Confidential record.** Tier 1, plus a confidential organization record in the program's data layer.
3. **Eligibility review.** Tier 2, plus review for sprint and red-team invitations.

You can withdraw from tiers 2 and 3 at any time. Withdrawal applies from that date forward.

## Confidentiality

- Your organization's identity and individual answers will not be disclosed without your written consent, except as required by Canadian law.
- Published results pass disclosure control: no published figure is based on fewer than the minimum number of organizations, and cells dominated by one or two organizations are suppressed.
- Open-text answers are never published word for word.
- All respondent-level data is stored and processed in Canada.

This is a voluntary private survey. It does not carry the statutory confidentiality protection of a government statistical survey; confidentiality rests on these terms, technical controls and governance.

## Intellectual property

Your responses do not transfer, license or waive any intellectual property rights. Information you mark *confidential commercial* is accessible only to named program staff under confidentiality agreements. It is never published and never shared with other consortium members.

## Export controls: no technical data

> I confirm my answers will not include technical data, drawings, specifications or performance data controlled under the Canadian Export Control List, the Controlled Goods Program, US ITAR or any other export-control regime.

Questions ask only for categories, ranges and yes or no answers against **public** thresholds. Open-text fields are screened automatically and will block numbers that look like specifications. Do not upload drawings, specifications, test data or performance values. Any such material received will be deleted.

Evidence uploads (optional) are limited to non-technical documents: certificates, audit letters, contract award notices and redacted summaries.
