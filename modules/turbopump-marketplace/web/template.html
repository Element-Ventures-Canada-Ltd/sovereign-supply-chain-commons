How to read this

Build No public supplier anywhere. A new Canadian capability, or an allied build-partner-buy deal.

Domesticate Foreign-owned suppliers only. Canadian investment, a joint venture, or qualifying a Canadian alternate.

Export-listed On Canada's Export Control List. Suppliers need Controlled Goods Program registration.
